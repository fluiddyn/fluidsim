# Related projects

There are many CFD framework and Fourier pseudo-spectral solvers. We just list here few examples:

## Open-source

- [Dedalus](https://dedalus-project.org/)

- [SpectralDNS](https://github.com/spectralDNS) (M. Mortensen)

- [TurTLE](https://turtle.pages.mpcdf.de/turtle/index.html)

- [GHOST](https://github.com/pmininni/GHOST) (P. Mininni)

- [HIT3D](https://github.com/schumakov/hit3d)

- [Tarang](https://github.com/cardich/tarang)

- [NS3D](https://archive.softwareheritage.org/browse/origin/directory/?origin_url=https://bitbucket.org/paugier/ns3d)

## Not open-source

- [GESTS](https://impact.ornl.gov/en/publications/gpu-enabled-extreme-scale-turbulence-simulations-fourier-pseudo-s/) (P.K. Yeung)
